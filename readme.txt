Split Email Providers is a WooCommerce addon that automatically sends emails to suppliers for each order, simplifying communication.

== Description ==

Save time and simplify your supplier order management on WooCommerce!

With Split Email Providers, each order is automatically sorted by supplier and a personalized email is sent with the product details they need to prepare.

✅ Perfect for dropshipping, multi-brand stores, and B2B sales
✅ No more manual emails or missed orders
✅ 100% WooCommerce compatible with a quick and easy setup

Why choose Split Email Providers?

Fully automated order email sending to your suppliers

Split orders by supplier without using a third-party marketplace

Ultra-simple setup in just a few clicks

Save 3 to 5 hours of manual work per week on your WooCommerce store.

Main features:

Automatic email sending to suppliers: With every order, emails are sent to the relevant suppliers with the details of their products.

Seamless integration: Works directly with WooCommerce without complicated configuration.

Split orders by supplier based on the products in each order.

== Installation ==

Download the addon from the WordPress repository or directly from our website.

Go to your WordPress dashboard, click “Plugins > Add New” and upload the file.

Go to WooCommerce > Split Email Providers to add your suppliers and their email addresses.

Assign suppliers to products, and automatically send emails to the relevant supplier for each order.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does it work with any supplier? =
Yes — you can associate as many suppliers as you want by simply adding their emails in the settings.

= Is this addon only for WooCommerce? =
Yes — Split Email Providers is designed specifically for WooCommerce stores.

= Does the addon send emails automatically? =
Yes — as soon as an order is placed and validated, the addon detects the supplier’s products and sends the emails automatically.

= Can the content of the supplier emails be customized? =
No — advanced email customization is coming very soon in the Pro version.
